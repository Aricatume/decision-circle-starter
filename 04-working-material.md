## Draft

## Before sharing

- Check factual statements against their sources.
- Label invented examples as fictional.
- Remove private information that is not needed for the purpose.
- Get permission before sharing a client's case or confidential material. Removing names alone does not establish permission.
- Read the final version and decide whether to share it.
