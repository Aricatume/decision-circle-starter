# Five questions to check your decision

Use these yourself, on paper or in a text editor. You do not need AI or a conversation with the author.

1. **Who needs the result?** Name the person or role and the work they need to do.
2. **What decision am I making?** Turn a broad goal into one question with alternatives.
3. **What do I actually know?** Point to the source of each observation. Keep explanations and unknowns separate.
4. **What else could I do?** Compare at least two distinct actions, including collecting information or waiting when appropriate.
5. **What small check comes next?** Name an action, its owner, a deadline and what you will observe when you return.

Read your answers against the original material. Agreement with a fluent explanation is not evidence that it is true. If an answer is missing, record the gap and choose whether it needs investigation before action.
