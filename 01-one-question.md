# One working question

Date:
Review date:

- Who needs the result of this work?
- What decision do I need to make, in one sentence?
- Why now? What deadline or event makes it relevant?
- What have I already tried, and what happened?
- What material do I have? Where can I find it again?
- Which actions are within my control?
- What will the recipient be able to see or use after this work?
- What is outside the scope of this task?

Write “unknown” where needed. Do not invent an answer to complete the page.
