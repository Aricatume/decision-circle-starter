# From notes to a decision

**Furniture of One’s Mind · Фурнитура ума**

Decision Circle — free starter. Version 0.1, 13 September 2026.

For an independent expert with a current client question, scattered notes and a next step to choose. This is a complete manual cycle: **question → decision map → small action → review**.

## Your first pass

1. Make a dated copy of [One working question](01-one-question.md). Choose one small, current task.
2. Fill in [Decision map](02-decision-map.md). Separate what you observed from what you assume. “I don’t know” is a useful answer.
3. Choose one action you can take and write down how you will check it.
4. Set a date for [Weekly review](03-weekly-review.md), usually about a week later. Add your own calendar reminder.
5. On that date, reopen your original map and record what actually happened. Keep the original expectation; do not rewrite it to fit the outcome.

You can try a 20-minute first pass: 3 minutes for the question, 10 for the map, 5 for the action and 2 for the review date. This is a suggested time budget, not a tested promise. If the task is too large, narrow it.

## What is in the folder

- [One working question](01-one-question.md)
- [Decision map](02-decision-map.md)
- [Weekly review](03-weekly-review.md)
- [Working material](04-working-material.md) — turn a decision into a usable paragraph or proposal
- [Fictional worked example](05-fictional-example.md) — including a clearly imagined review
- [Five questions](06-five-questions.md) — check your reasoning yourself

Open this folder as an Obsidian vault, or edit the Markdown files in any text editor. No AI, account, plugins or internet connection is required. Save your own dated working copies so that the blank templates remain reusable. The files do not create reminders, send messages or check facts automatically.

This is an early author-made tool. Its usefulness for customers has not been established. A completed map is a record of your reasoning, not proof that the decision is correct or will earn money.
