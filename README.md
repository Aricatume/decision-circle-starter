# Decision Circle — Free Markdown Starter

Seven Markdown files for independent experts working through a current client question, scattered notes and a choice about what to do next. The starter provides a manual sequence: question, decision, action and review. You write the content and check its sources. Begin with the [start guide](00-start-here.md).

## Four steps

1. **Frame one question.** Make a dated copy of [One working question](01-one-question.md). Identify who needs the result, what choice is in scope and which actions are within your control.
2. **Record a decision.** Use the [Decision map](02-decision-map.md) to separate observations, sources, assumptions and unknowns. Compare distinct options, including waiting or collecting information when appropriate.
3. **Take one action.** Name its owner, deadline and completion criterion. Keep the effect you want to observe separate from completing the task. The [Working material](04-working-material.md) template provides space for a proposal, note or test description.
4. **Return and review.** Set your own reminder, then reopen the original map and complete [Weekly review](03-weekly-review.md). Record what happened without rewriting the earlier expectation to match the outcome.

The [fictional worked example](05-fictional-example.md) illustrates the sequence, including an explicitly invented later review. Its people, notes, numbers and outcomes are not customer evidence. [Five questions](06-five-questions.md) provides a separate checklist for examining your reasoning.

Open the folder in Obsidian or edit the files in any text editor. Keep blank templates and save dated working copies. No account, plugins, AI or internet connection is required to use the downloaded files. They do not create reminders, verify facts or publish anything automatically.

Write “unknown” when information is missing. A completed map does not establish that a decision is correct, useful or commercially successful. Usefulness for customers and scientific effectiveness have not been established.
